Your resume is usually the first — and sometimes only — chance to get a US hiring manager's attention. Recruiters often spend less than a minute on the first scan. These tips help international full-stack developers make that minute count.

## 1. Start with a sharp summary

Two or three lines at the top that answer: who are you, how experienced are you and what do you build?

> Full-stack developer with 5 years of experience building SaaS products with React, Next.js, Node.js and PostgreSQL. Experienced working remotely with distributed teams across time zones.

## 2. Put your stack where it can't be missed

Add a short skills section grouped by category — frontend, backend, databases, cloud and tooling. Only list technologies you'd be comfortable being interviewed on.

## 3. Show impact with numbers

US companies care about outcomes. Turn each bullet into a result:

- "Built a checkout flow in Next.js and Stripe that increased conversion by 18%"
- "Reduced API response times from 900ms to 200ms with query optimization and caching"
- "Led the migration of a legacy PHP app to Node.js and TypeScript for 30k monthly users"

## 4. Highlight remote and international experience

Have you worked with clients in other countries, or on a distributed team? Say so explicitly. It reassures US employers that you know how to communicate asynchronously and handle time-zone differences.

## 5. Keep it to one or two pages

One page for up to about five years of experience; two pages maximum beyond that. Cut older or less relevant roles down to a single line.

## 6. Leave out personal details

US resumes do **not** include a photo, date of birth, marital status, nationality or a full home address. City and country are enough. Including these details can actually hurt you, because many US companies are trained to avoid them.

## 7. Link to proof

Add your LinkedIn, GitHub and portfolio links near the top. A clean GitHub profile with a couple of well-documented projects is one of the strongest signals you can send.

## 8. Write it in natural English and send a PDF

Have someone proofread it, keep the formatting simple and save it as a PDF so it looks the same everywhere. Name the file clearly, for example `Maria-Silva-Full-Stack-Developer.pdf`.
